# Personas
There are the following personas for the GameSetMatch application:

- Tour Administrator
- Tournament Director
- Player
- Umpire
- Tennis Fan
- GameSetMatch Administrator

## Tour Administrator Persona
The Tour Administrator is responsible for defining the structure of the tennis tour and turning it into a practical schedule of tournaments for each season. They define which tournaments exist on the tour, maintain key tournament details such as dates, locations, venue details, competition level, and the events included in the tournament. They ensure that each tournament fits logically within the wider calendar, avoiding scheduling conflicts. They assign tournament directors to each tournament in the season.

Their primary goal is to create a coherent season. They need clear visibility of the full tour calendar, reliable tournament data, conflict alerts, flexible scheduling tools, and confidence that any changes are communicated consistently to all stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Set and maintain tournament details, including dates, locations, competition level, surface, and the included events.
- Define which tournaments are included in the tour for each season.
- Define the details of the venues that can host tournaments
- Schedule tournaments into a coherent season calendar and assign a venue to them.
- Assign tournament directors to each tournament.
- Make changes to the schedule when necessary, having these changes communicated to all stakeholders such as tournament directors, players, and umpires.

## Tournament Director Persona

The Tournament Director is responsible for running an individual tennis tournament from preparation through to completion. Each tournament can have a number of They manage the practical delivery of the tournament, ensuring that the right players are invited or accepted, events are organised correctly, draws are conducted fairly, and matches are scheduled in a way that makes effective use of courts, officials, and available time.

In this role, the director balances competitive fairness with day-to-day operational constraints. They invite players to the tournament, review and process player applications, confirm entries for each event, and manage any withdrawals or late changes. Once entries are confirmed, they conduct the draw for the relevant events and create a match schedule that assigns each match to a suitable court with an umpire.

### Responsibilities

- Invite eligible players to participate in the tournament.
- Define the number of entries and number of seeds for each event
- Review, accept, reject, or waitlist player applications for tournament entry.
- Confirm the final player list for each event within the tournament.
- Manage withdrawals, substitutions, late entries, and other entry changes.
- Conduct fair and accurate draws for the singles, and doubles events.
- Create the match schedule and sequence matches across the tournament days.
- Assign each match to an appropriate court based on availability
- Assign umpires to matches and coordinate officiating coverage throughout the tournament.
- Communicate draws, match times, court assignments, and schedule updates to players, and officials.

## Player Persona

The Player is a participant who wants to join the tennis tour and compete in tournament events across the season. They request to join the tour, provide the information needed for their application, and wait for their application to be reviewed before they can enter eligible tournament events.

Once part of the tour, the Player can be invited to play in an event at a tournament or can apply directly to enter an event where applications are open. They need to understand which tournament events they have entered, whether their entry has been accepted, and what their upcoming match commitments are. They are informed about upcoming matches, including the scheduled match time and assigned court, so they can prepare and arrive at the correct place on time.

### Responsibilities

- Request to join the tennis tour and provide any required application information.
- Respond to invitations to play in tournament events.
- Apply to enter tournament events where applications are open.
- Track which tournament events they have entered and the status of each entry.
- Review confirmed tournament entries, so they know where they are expected to compete.
- Receive updates about upcoming matches, including match time and assigned court.
- Stay informed about changes to event entries, draws, match times, or court assignments.
- Withdraw from a match if unable to start the match
- View details of their match history and ranking history
- Manage their profile
- Retire from the tour

## Umpire Persona

The Umpire is an official who registers their availability to officiate at tennis tournaments. They support the fair running of matches by accepting officiating assignments, attending the correct court at the scheduled time, and managing the match according to the rules of the competition.

In this role, the Umpire is notified when they have been assigned to officiate a match, including the match time and court. During the match, they keep score accurately, manage match progress, and ensure that the result is recorded once the match has finished.

Their work helps tournament directors maintain an accurate schedule, reliable match records, and a smooth tournament operation.

### Responsibilities

- Register to be available as an official for each tournament.
- Receive notifications for assigned matches, including match time and court.
- Prepare to officiate matches based on the latest tournament schedule.
- Attend the assigned court at the scheduled time.
- Officiate the match in line with tournament rules and procedures.
- Keep an accurate score throughout the match.
- Record the outcome of the match once play has finished.
- Stay informed about changes to match assignments, court allocations, or start times.
- Manage their profile
- Retire from the tour

## Tennis Fan Persona 

The Tennis Fan is a member of the public who wants to follow the tennis tour throughout the season. They use the public fan website to discover tournaments, understand when and where they are taking place, and keep up with the players and matches that interest them.

The Tennis Fan needs clear, up-to-date information about tournament schedules, upcoming matches, matches currently in progress, completed results, player profiles, and rankings. They want to move easily between tour-wide information and the detail for a particular tournament, match, or player so they can follow the competition as it develops.

### Responsibilities

- View the current tour schedule and browse tournaments across the season.
- View tournament details, including dates, location, venue, surface, competition level, and included events.
- See upcoming matches, including scheduled time, court, event, round, and competing players.
- Follow matches currently in progress and view the latest score and match status.
- View completed match results and tournament draws.
- Browse player profiles, including current ranking, ranking history, and match history.
- Follow selected tournaments, matches, or players to find relevant information quickly.
- Stay informed about schedule changes, delays, court changes, and other match updates.

## GameSetMatch Administrator

The GameSetMatch Administrator is responsible for managing access to the system and ensuring that users have the correct roles assigned to them. They support the operational setup of the tour by inviting new users, assigning them appropriate roles, and maintaining accurate user and player records.

In this role, the Administrator acts as a central control point for user administration tasks. They invite users such as tour administrators, tournament directors, and umpires, to the system, assign roles that determine what each user can access or manage,

They review player applications to join the tour. Their work helps ensure that the right people can use the system at the right time, with access that matches their responsibilities.

### Responsibilities

- Invite new users to access the system.
- Assign users to the appropriate system roles based on their responsibilities.
- Update or remove user access when roles or responsibilities change.
- Review player applications to join the tour.
- Accept, or reject player tour applications.
- Ensure approved players are correctly set up in the system and available for tournament entry.
- Support secure and consistent access management across the tour administration process.
