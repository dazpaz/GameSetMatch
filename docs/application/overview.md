# The GameSetMatch Application Overview
Game Set Match is an integrated tennis-tour platform that supports the full season lifecycle: tour and venue setup, tournament planning, player entry, draws and match scheduling, officiating and scoring, results, rankings, and public tour coverage.

Its four applications provide role-appropriate experiences while sharing consistent tournament, match, player, and user information.

## Applications
- **Admin Console**: Used by administrators, tour administrators, umpires, and tournament directors to manage users and roles, players, venues, tournaments, the season calendar, entries, draws, match schedules, and tournament completion.
- **Player Portal**: Enables players to apply to join the tour, manage their profiles, respond to invitations, enter events, track entry status, view upcoming matches and notifications, and review rankings and match history.
- **Umpire App**: Enables umpires to view assignments, follow schedule changes, start and officiate matches, keep score, record results, and manage their profiles.
- **Public Fan Website**: Gives tennis fans public access to the tour calendar, tournament and venue details, upcoming and active matches, results, draws, player profiles, and rankings.

Personas
- **Tennis Tour Administrator**: Defines tournaments and venues, builds the season schedule, resolves calendar conflicts, and assigns tournament directors.
- **Tennis Tournament Director**: Runs an individual tournament, manages entries and withdrawals, conducts draws, schedules matches, and coordinates courts and officials.
- **Player**: Applies to join the tour, enters tournament events, tracks entry status, prepares for scheduled matches, and receives relevant updates.
- **Umpire**: Registers availability, accepts match assignments, officiates play, keeps score, and records match outcomes.
- **Tennis Fan**: Follows the tour through tournament information, upcoming and live matches, results, draws, player profiles, and rankings.
- **GameSetMatch Administrator**: Manages system access, invitations, roles, and player applications to ensure users and players are correctly set up.
