# Umpire Portal
The main purpose of the umpire Portal is to allow umpires to officiate a tennis match and keep score. Umpires use the portal to: 

- Login to the umpire portal
- Logout from the umpire portal
- See their home page
- View list of tournaments they are registered to umpire at
- See their scheduled matches
- Start a match
- Umpire a match, keeping the match score
- Complete a match, declaring the winner
- Manage their profile

Umpires also have access to the [Admin Console](admin-console.md)
